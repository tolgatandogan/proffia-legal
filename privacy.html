Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data Proffia ("we", "us") collects when you use the Proffia mobile app, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

1. Data We Collect

DataWhy
The photo you uploadTo generate your requested AI images
Generated imagesTo deliver and let you re-download your results
An anonymous account identifierTo track your credits and generation history across sessions, without requiring an email or phone number
Purchase/subscription statusTo grant credits and manage your subscription, via RevenueCat and Apple/Google
Language preferenceStored on your device only, to show the App in your chosen language

We do not require you to create an account with an email or password. By default, Proffia signs you in with an anonymous identity that is not linked to your real-world identity unless you choose to provide one (e.g. an email, if a sign-in method requiring one is ever added).

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To process your uploaded photo and generate the images you request.
  • To track and enforce your credit balance and subscription status.
  • To improve reliability and fix bugs, using error/crash information.

We do not use your uploaded photos to train AI models, and we do not sell your data to third parties or use it for third-party advertising.

3. Third Parties We Use

To operate the App, uploaded photos and generation requests pass through the following service providers, each acting under their own privacy/security terms:

  • Google (Gemini AI service) — your uploaded selfie and the template photo you chose are sent to Google's Gemini image-generation API to create your image. This is the step that processes your face photo with a third-party AI service; the App asks for your explicit consent before your first upload.
  • Modal — runs the server function that prepares and relays the generation request.
  • Cloudflare (Workers and R2 storage) — handles the App's API and stores uploaded photos and generated images.
  • Supabase — stores your anonymous account, credit balance, and generation history.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription and purchase state.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — process payments; Proffia never sees your card details.

We do not use your photos to train AI models and do not sell them. You can withdraw consent at any time by not uploading further photos and by requesting deletion of your data (Section 6).

4. Data Retention

We retain your uploaded photos, generated images, and account data for as long as your account is active, so you can access your generation history. You can request deletion at any time (Section 6).

5. Children's Privacy

Proffia is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the App, contact us and we will delete the associated data.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data. In the App, open Settings and tap “Delete my data” to send us a prefilled deletion request. You can also email phantomgamelab@gmail.com — we'll act on verified requests within a reasonable time.

7. International Transfers

Our service providers may process and store data in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on those providers' own compliance mechanisms (such as standard contractual clauses) for cross-border transfers.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, access-controlled storage) to protect your data. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email phantomgamelab@gmail.com.
