Common questions

  • My subscription didn't restore. Use "Restore Purchases" in the app's Support & Legal screen, signed in with the same Apple/Google account you purchased with.
  • I want to cancel my subscription. Subscriptions are managed by the App Store or Google Play, not by Proffia directly — cancel from your device's account/subscription settings.
  • I want my data deleted. Email us at the address above and we'll process the request.
